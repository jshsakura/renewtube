RenewTube

A Simple, Quiet YouTube Player

Turns YouTube into a player of your own. Music and video on one screen, and YouTube still does the playing.

Download GitHub

One package works across Orion, Chrome, Edge, and compatible browsers.

Features

Core Experience

A player screen instead of the recommendation feed. YouTube keeps the streams and the data; only the screen changes.

Music First

The first screen is music: shelves of playlists and charts, filled without a Google sign-in.

Queue & Playlists

See what plays next and reorder it. Shuffle, repeat and playlists live here too.

Music & Video Modes

A track list for listening, a thumbnail grid for watching. One button switches between them.

Zero Shorts

Shorts appear in no shelf and no search result.

Keyboard & Remote

Arrow keys move between items and Enter opens them, the way a TV remote does.

A Menu You Choose

Home, Subscriptions, History and Playlists, plus the TV menu lines: Sports, Live, Gaming, News, Learning, Kids. Switch on only what you want.

Search That Helps

Suggestions as you type, recent searches remembered, and playlists and channels among the results.

Desktop & Mobile

Works on desktop Chrome, Orion and Edge, and on Orion for iPhone and iPad.

Installation

How to Install

Register the downloaded package with your browser. Setup completes in under a minute without complex configuration.

Orion Browser

  1. Click the Download button above to save the zip package.
  2. Open Settings → Extensions in Orion and tap + to select the downloaded zip file.
  3. Click the extension icon in the toolbar to activate RenewTube.

Chrome & Edge

  1. Unzip the downloaded package into your preferred folder.
  2. Navigate to chrome://extensions in the address bar and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked at the top left and select the extracted folder.

Good to Know

Design Principles & Notes

Transparent details on how RenewTube operates within the browser.

Double-Esc to Exit Anytime
Leaves YouTube intact. Press Esc twice within 1 second to close RenewTube and get the ordinary YouTube page back.
Background & Screen-Off Playback
Playback with the screen turned off relies on native browser background audio capabilities (such as Orion's background playback).
No Built-In Ad Blocking
RenewTube complies with YouTube streaming standards and does not block ads. Dedicated content blockers can be used alongside it.
Library Requires Sign-In
Search, music and the TV menu work signed out. Subscriptions, history and playlists appear once you sign in to YouTube.
